**The townsfolk (36 named people, each with their own 3D model):**

  the tattooist, Agatha the cat keeper, Isadora the painter, Gideon the toymaker.
- Notables: Governor Thorne (sells a PARDON if you crossed the Armada), Harbourmaster Quill, Martha the innkeeper,
  Silas the lighthouse keeper (works nights), Father Anselm, Brannoc the blacksmith, Big Hal of the Grog Barrel.
- Country folk: Hob the farmer, Elspeth the shepherdess, Cobb the cider maker, Wilma the miller.
- Lore: Madame Zora (fortunes, 3 doubloons - sometimes names your next boss), Ned Barlow (Captain Wren's first mate),
  Rufus Brine (ask for a TALE - a story about the boss you face next).
- Townsfolk: Finn, Nell, Jack, Commodore Pettigrew, Rosalind, Fiddler Dan, Ptolemy the astronomer, Morwenna.
- NEW (round 2, they just talk, most lodge at the inn): Harmonia the organist, Pip + Molly (Martha's children -
  they play on the park swings), Tobias the TOWN CRIER (walks a round and shouts REAL news: rare birds in the aviary,
  the next service, last night's chess result, who finished a painting, rain), Ginny the lamplighter (her round is at
  dusk), Sergeant Brask (patrols the north road), Agnes who feeds the gulls (Should we add seagulls to make this real), Seraphine the mysterious traveller.

**Their day:** work by day -> evenings: drink at the Grog Barrel, dice / crown & anchor / roulette in the den, CHESS
against each other (real games, both boards), PAINTING at the easels (it fills in on the canvas, then they hang it on
their own wall at home), the chapel, a stroll, the kids on the swings -> home -> bed.

**The market keepers** (7 traders + the Map Merchant): booth by day, the inn's common room from dusk (sat on the
stools - you can still trade), an inn bed of their own at night.

**THE CHURCH SERVICE:** every third morning after the bell (about 7-9 am). The pray-ers fill the pews, Father Anselm
preaches from the lectern (his sermon appears in chat if you're in the chapel), Harmonia plays Amazing Grace on the
organ at the start and the end. On other mornings she practises (softly, on the flute stop).

## IDEAS - mark each one: [x] yes / [ ] no / add notes

### Life in town
- [Yes] **Gossip that's true**: townsfolk mention what really happened ("Gideon beat the Commodore at chess last night",
      "Rosalind's new painting is lovely", "the captain who killed the Kraken was in here!") - the crier already does
      some of this; spread it to everyone's chat lines.
- [Yes] **Weather**: when it rains they hurry indoors, the market keepers put up awnings, Rosalind opens her parasol.
- [Yes] **Music night**: Fiddler Dan plays in the Grog Barrel on some evenings (a real tune, like the organ) and the
      drinkers get up and dance.
- [Yes but make it a true tiny pop up store] **Market day**: once a week extra stalls appear in the plaza (a travelling merchant with rare stock), more
      people about.
- [Yes] **Festivals**: a few times a "month" - lanterns strung up, fireworks over the harbour at night, everyone out.
- [Yes] **Celebrate the player**: when you kill a chain boss, the town throws a party next evening - fireworks, the
      crier announces it, people congratulate you when you talk to them.
- [Yes] **Ship comes in**: when you dock a big ship, a few townsfolk wander down to the quay to look at it.
- [Yes] **Fishermen fish**: Finn actually casts from the pier (bobber in the water) and brings fish to the Fishmonger.
- [Yes] **Night watch**: soldiers walk the walls and streets at night with lanterns; Ginny lights real lamps at dusk
      (lamps that are off by day and lit at night).
- [Yes] **Children's games**: Pip and Molly play tag / hide-and-seek around the park, and run up to you to ask about
      your ship. Maybe we add a few more kids too.
- [Yes] **Pets**: a few townsfolk have a cat or a parrot that follows them about.

### Relationships with the player
- [Yes] **Friendship**: talk to people, buy from them, give gifts -> they like you more -> small discounts, extra
      dialogue, they wave when you pass.
- [No] **Small favours** (not full quests): "bring me 10 wool", "find my lost locket on the beach" -> doubloons,
      a unique decorative item, or a friendship boost.
- [Yes] **Buy your paintings**: Isadora (or a gallery in town) buys good player paintings, or hangs them in a public
      gallery with your name on.
- [Yes] **Chess challenge**: challenge a townsperson to chess (they sit opposite you; each has their own strength -
      beat the Commodore for a trophy).
- [Yes] **Dice with the regulars**: sit at the den tables and the NPC gamblers actually play against you and remember the games.

### More people
- [Yes] **A doctor / surgeon** (patches you up for doubloons, sells bandages, has a peg-leg collection).
- [No] **A schoolteacher** + a few more children (a little schoolroom - the kids go to school in the morning).
- [Yes] **A night-time smuggler** who only appears after dark behind the warehouses with odd stock.
- [Yes] **Fort soldiers** - a handful of named guards at the fort and the gates.
- [No] **A married couple** who argue and make up (they go to the tavern together, sit together in chapel).
- [No] **Your own crew**: hire named townsfolk as crew for your ship (they'd need a bed at the inn when ashore).

### The chapel
- [Yes] **Weddings** (two townsfolk, or the player!) and a **memorial** service when a player dies at sea (their name on
      the memorial plaque).
- [Yes] **Choir**: two or three townsfolk sing along to the hymns (note particles over their heads).
- [Yes] **More hymns** for the organ (all public domain - e.g. Eternal Father Strong to Save, the sailors' hymn), can one of the songs be a version of Thick of it by KSI.
