## Built from part 1 (2026-10-04/05 - try them in game)
- **Gossip that's true**: everyone mentions real news (chess results, paintings, boss kills, weddings, the best catch,
  ships in, dice wins); the crier shouts it.
- **Weather**: in the rain the outdoor folk shelter in the inn + tavern; Ginny and the night watch carry on.
- **Music night** (every other evening): Dan fiddles in the Grog Barrel (Drunken Sailor + our own jig), people dance.
- **Market day** (every 7th day): a tiny pop-up stall in the plaza + Marco Venn the travelling merchant, new rare stock
  each visit.
- **Festivals** (every 8th day) + **the party for you** (the evening after you kill a chain boss): lanterns + bunting
  round the plaza, everyone out dancing, Dan playing, fireworks over the harbour.
- **Ships in**: when your ship is moored in the harbour, townsfolk wander down to the quay to look at it.
- **Finn fishes** off the pier (splashes, catches - the best one makes the news).
- **The night watch**: Corporal Hale + Private Finch walk the town all night with lanterns. **STREET LAMPS**: Ginny lights
  them on her dusk round (old lamppost lanterns turn into street lamps as she passes); they go out by morning.
- **Children's games**: Pip, Molly, Tom and Bella play tag in the Gardens, ride the swings, and run up to ask about your
  ship. (Tom + Bella are new - foundlings Martha took in.)
- **Pets**: Agatha's cat Marmalade, the Commodore's cat Admiral, Silas's dog Biscuit, Elspeth's sheepdog Shep, Hob's dog
  Barley, Rufus's parrot Captain Squawk follow their people about.
- **SEAGULLS**: wheel over the harbour + plaza, land to strut and peck; Agnes's crumbs bring them all down.
- **Friendship**: talk daily, trade, give gifts (everyone has favourite things - a liked gift counts much more) ->
  Acquaintance / Friend / Good Friend / Best Friend; friends wave, greet you by name, and give 5-15% off. Shown on the
  dialogue card.
- **The gallery**: Isadora buys your own paintings (better = more colour, more painted) and hangs them in her studio.
- **Chess challenge**: ask anyone who plays chess for a game - they sit opposite, you take white. Beat Commodore
  Pettigrew for **The Commodore's Chess Trophy**.
- **Dice with the regulars**: the townsfolk gambling at a Liar's Dice table take the bot seats, and remember the score.
- **Dr Elias Marrow** the surgeon (heals you + clears bad effects for doubloons, sells bandages and tonics),
  **Lazlo Quick** the smuggler (only after dark, in the alley between the warehouses, odd stock), **the fort guards**
  (Lieutenant Ashby + Private Dobbs on the gates by day, Hale + Finch by night).
- **Weddings**: every 12th day a townsfolk couple marry (Cobb + Wilma first); two PLAYERS can ask Father Anselm to marry
  them any time (stand at the altar together). Bridal Chorus on the organ, vows, bells, confetti, fireworks.
- **Memorials**: if you die at sea, your name goes on the memorial roll (read the chapel's memorial plaques) and there's
  a memorial service next morning.
- **The choir**: some of the congregation sing along to the hymns. **More hymns**: Joyful, Joyful (Ode to Joy) and the
  Bridal Chorus. Not added: "Thick of It" (KSI) - it's copyrighted, so Dan plays our own jig, "Salt and Thunder",
  in its place. "Eternal Father, Strong to Save" can come later if you send me the tune.
